-**Scaling:** 
    Scaling is the process of increasing an application's capacity so it can handle a larger number of users and requests.

-**Vertical Scaling:** 
    Vertical scaling is the process of increasing the resources of a single server, such as **CPU, RAM, or storage**, to handle more requests.

-**Horizontal Scaling:** 
    Horizontal scaling is the process of adding **more servers** to an application and distributing requests among them, usually with the help of a **load balancer**.
